# LawyerLink Product Requirements Document (PRD) ## Project Overview LawyerLink is a modern SaaS legal marketplace connecting clients with lawyers, powered by AI recommendations. The platform aims to demystify the process of finding legal help by analyzing user problems and matching them with specialized professionals. ## User Roles & Key Features ### 1. Client * **AI Consultation:** Describe legal issues to an AI assistant for categorization and lawyer recommendations. * **Discovery:** Browse and filter a directory of verified lawyers. * **Booking:** Schedule and manage consultations. * **Dashboard:** Track upcoming appointments, AI history, and favorite lawyers. ### 2. Lawyer * **Profile Management:** Professional bio, license verification, and specialty listing. * **Lead Management:** Accept/reject consultation requests. * **Growth Tools:** Subscription management and "Boost Visibility" features. * **Dashboard:** Earnings overview, meeting schedule, and reviews summary. ### 3. Admin * **Platform Oversight:** User and lawyer management, payment monitoring. * **Moderation:** Review and reservation monitoring. * **Analytics:** Growth metrics, revenue tracking, and AI log analysis. ## Design Specifications ### Visual Style * **Aesthetic:** Clean, minimal, professional, and trustworthy. * **Components:** Card-based layouts, large white space, subtle shadows, rounded corners. * **Typography:** Inter or Poppins (Modern Sans-Serif). * **Color Palette:** * Primary: Dark Blue (#1E3A8A) * Secondary: Blue (#3B82F6) * Accent: Gold (#F59E0B) * Background: White / Light Gray ### Layout Structure * **Public:** Top navigation (Logo, Find Lawyers, AI Assistant, Auth). * **Dashboard:** Sidebar navigation with a clean content area and top utility bar. ## Roadmap of Screens 1. **Landing Page:** Conversion-focused hero and features. 2. **Auth (Login/Sign Up):** Role-based registration for Clients and Lawyers. 3. **Lawyers Directory:** Advanced filtering and grid view. 4. **Lawyer Profile:** Detailed bio, credentials, and reviews. 5. **AI Legal Assistant:** Chat interface with recommended lawyer cards. 6. **Client Dashboard:** Personal activity and appointment tracking. 7. **Lawyer Dashboard:** Business management and analytics. 8. **Booking Page:** Calendar and time-slot selection. 9. **Messaging:** Secure client-lawyer communication. 10. **Notifications:** Real-time updates for platform activity. 11. **Lawyer Subscription:** Tiered pricing for professionals. 12. **Boost Visibility:** Performance metrics and ad-buy options. 13. **Admin Dashboard:** High-level platform management.
